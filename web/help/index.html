Single Shot

Help

How to play, common questions, and how to reach us.

How to play

  1. Drag to aim the launcher.
  2. Release to fire. The orb banks off walls.
  3. Pop every target with that one shot to clear the room.
  4. Missed? The room resets. Try again as many times as you like.
  5. Clear it on your first try for ★★★.

Questions

What does "one shot" mean? Can I retry?

Each attempt is a single shot: one orb that has to pop every target in the room. If it misses, the room resets so you can try again. Tries are unlimited.

How do stars work?
  • ★★★ first try, without a hint.
  • ★★ within three tries, or first try with a hint.
  • ★ any clear after that.
What are the dots above the launcher?

They show how many bounces your shot may make in this room. Plan your angle so the orb reaches every target within that many bounces.

How do hints work?

You get 3 free hints a day, refilled at midnight in your local time. If you're out, the Android app offers an optional short video to earn one more hint. It always asks first, and if no video is available the hint is free.

How do I unlock the next chapter?

Clear 15 rooms in a chapter to open the next one. There are 9 chapters of 40 rooms, 360 rooms in total.

What's new in each chapter?
  • DawnPlain walls. Learn the bank shot.
  • GlassGlass panes break after one bounce.
  • GateHit the switch to dissolve the gates.
  • DriftPlatforms never stop sliding: time your release.
  • TurnPivot walls turn 90° when hit.
  • ShiftPortals send the orb somewhere else.
  • VoidDark blocks swallow the orb; round bumpers bounce it.
  • PrismPrisms split the orb; ringed targets need two passes.
  • SparkFlying through a spark adds a bounce.
What can I buy, and how do I restore purchases?

All purchases are optional and one-time (no subscriptions), through Google Play on Android:

  • Complete pack: everything below.
  • Supporter pack: no banner ads, and every hint is free.
  • Level keys: open any locked room for good (₹30 each, 10% off for 5 or more; or watch a video for a free key, up to 5 a day).
  • Orb pack: extra orb styles and trails.
  • Theme pack: extra colour themes.
  • Tip jar: a thank-you, if you'd like.

Purchases belong to your Google account. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open the shop and tap Restore purchases.

I bought something but it didn't unlock
  1. Check you're online and signed in to the same Google account you bought with.
  2. Open the shop and tap Restore purchases.
  3. Close the game completely and open it again.
  4. Payments can stay "pending" for a while; it unlocks once Google Play confirms it.

Still stuck? Contact support with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and we'll sort it out.

Ads: what will I see, and how do I remove them?

On Android there's a small banner at the bottom, plus reward videos that only play when you choose to watch one (for a hint, a level key, or a one-hour try of the Orb or Theme pack). After every 10 rooms you clear, one full-screen ad may show when you tap Next, never in the middle of a room. The Supporter pack or Complete pack removes all ads and makes every hint free. Where consent applies, you can change it any time with Settings → Ad privacy choices. The web version has no ads. See the Privacy policy for details.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not at the moment. There's no account or cloud save, so progress and stars are stored only on the device you play on. Purchases do carry over: use Restore purchases on the new phone.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings → Reset progress. This clears your rooms and stars on this device and can't be undone. Purchases aren't affected.

What's the difference between the web and Android versions?

Same rooms and rules. The web version has no ads and no purchases, and saves progress in your browser, so clearing site data erases it. The Android app adds the optional shop and shows ads.

The game is too hard. Any tips?
  • Count the bounce dots first: the solution makes exactly that many bounces. Then trace the path backwards from the last target.
  • Tap Restart for a fresh start at try 1, with all three stars on offer again.
  • After a miss, the room shows your last path faintly. Nudge from there.
  • Use a hint. You get 3 free every day.
  • Explore: 15 clears open the next chapter, so a tough room late in a chapter never blocks you.
