Playing Ringburst

The basics

You are the glowing core in the middle. Hold anywhere on the screen and a ring grows outward from the core; let go and it bursts outward as a shockwave that hits every shape inside the ring. The faint dashed circle shows how big the ring can get. While the ring grows you aren't attacking, so a big ring is a gamble: more reach, but the shapes get closer.

A shape that touches the core breaks one of your shields (the blue pips around the core). Lose all three and the level is lost; tap Try again to have another go. You can revive once per level by watching a short video (Supporters revive without one).

PERFECT

When a shape sits right on the ring's edge, the ring turns white, the shape gets a white halo and a soft blip plays. Let go then for a PERFECT: the whole burst hits twice as hard, a spark jumps to a nearby shape, time slows for a moment and your combo goes up. The combo multiplies your score up to ×4. A burst that hits nothing breaks the combo, and so does a hit on the core.

Levels and tasks

Every level has one task, shown on a card before it starts: burst a number of shapes, land a number of PERFECTs, survive for a number of seconds, reach a combo, or burst a number of the world's new shape. Levels 10, 20 and 30 of each world bring a Giant, a big version of the world's new shape, and level 40 brings the world's boss. Finish the task to clear the level and open the next one. Most levels take 15 to 45 seconds.

Worlds and bosses

There are 8 worlds of 40 levels, 320 in all. Each world brings one new shape, shown before its first level: dashers lunge, wardens carry shields that only a PERFECT breaks, splitters burst into minis, orbiters spiral, hatchers park far away and breed, phantoms fade out of reach, and brutes are armoured. From the third world on, shapes take two plain hits or one PERFECT. Beat the boss at level 40 (Monolith, Lancer, Bastion, Hydra, Carousel, Hive Queen, Wraith and Eclipse) to open the next world.

Stars and awards

You earn one star for every shield you still have when the task is done, so clearing a level without being hit gives three stars. A level cleared after a revive gives one star. Replay a level any time to earn more. Awards are badges for milestones, like your first PERFECT or beating a world.

Shop (Android)

Every level opens by playing. The shop sells optional extras: the Complete pack (everything in one), the Supporter pack (no banner ads, and revives without a video), the Theme pack (11 colour themes), Ring styles (6 looks for your ring), and a tip jar. Themes and ring styles can be tried free for an hour with a video. Purchases go through Google Play; use Restore purchases after reinstalling.

Sharing

After a level, tap Share on the result card to send a picture of it (the level, its task, your score and stars) with a link to the game, or Save it to your gallery.

Saving

Progress saves automatically on your device. In the browser, progress is stored by your browser for this site; clearing site data erases it. Nothing is uploaded. If you played the earlier version of Ringburst, your settings and purchases carry over, but level progress starts again because the levels are new.

Controls

Touch or mouse: hold and release anywhere. Keyboard: hold and release Space; Esc pauses or goes back. On Android, the Back button pauses a level and works everywhere else. Screen shake and vibration can be turned off in Settings.

Purchases
