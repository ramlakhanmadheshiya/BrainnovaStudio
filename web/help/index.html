Snug Fit

Help

How to play, common questions, and how to reach us.

How to play

  1. Drag a piece from the tray into the shape.
  2. Fill every cell. Pieces can't overlap or stick out.
  3. Changed your mind? Drag a piece off the shape to take it back.
  4. Where pieces can turn, tap one to turn it a quarter. Turning in the tray is free.
  5. Every drop on the shape is a move. Finish near par (one move per piece) for ★★★.

Questions

How do stars work?
  • ★★★ filled in par moves, or just a little over, without a hint.
  • ★★ a few more moves, or with a hint.
  • ★ any finish.

The MOVES and STARS chips over the shape show what's still on offer. Restart is a fresh start with all three stars back.

Does every level have an answer?

Yes. Every one of the 210 levels was built with its answer and checked by a solver, which also makes sure there are very few other ways to fill it. Late in a chapter there is usually exactly one.

What's new in each chapter?
  • FitFill the shape. Pieces keep their angle.
  • TurnTap a piece to turn it.
  • PaintA coloured cell only takes the piece of its colour.
  • PegsEvery piece covers exactly one peg.
  • SpareOne piece too many: one stays in the tray.
  • FlipPieces turn over too: touch one, then press Flip.
  • MosaicEverything at once.
How do I unlock the next chapter?

Clear 12 levels in a chapter to open the next one. There are 7 chapters of 30 levels. A chapter key opens a locked chapter straight away, and a level key opens any one locked level.

How do hints and coins work?

A hint outlines exactly where the next piece goes (and moves any wrong pieces back to the tray first). Your first hint each day is free (3 a day for Supporters); after that a hint costs 30 coins.

You start with 150 coins and earn more by playing: +5 for clearing a level the first time, +5 for every new star, and +100 for finishing a chapter. In the Android app you can also watch a short video for +25 coins (up to 5 a day). Out of coins with no video available? The hint is free.

What can I buy, and how do I restore purchases?

All purchases are optional, through Google Play on Android, with no subscriptions:

  • Complete pack: everything below, plus 1,000 coins and 5 level keys.
  • Supporter pack: no ads, and 3 free hints every day.
  • Theme pack: 11 colour themes for the whole game.
  • Block styles: 6 more looks for every piece (Candy, Paper, Glass, Neon, Stone, Pixel).
  • Coins: bags of 300 to 6,000.
  • Chapter keys and level keys: open locked chapters or levels for good (or watch a video for one).
  • Tip jar: a thank-you, if you'd like.

The Theme pack and Block styles can each be tried for an hour from a video. Packs belong to your Google account: on a new phone or after reinstalling, open the shop and tap Restore purchases.

I bought something but it didn't unlock
  1. Check you're online and signed in to the same Google account you bought with.
  2. Open the shop and tap Restore purchases.
  3. Close the game completely and open it again.
  4. Payments can stay "pending" for a while; it unlocks once Google Play confirms it.

Still stuck? Contact support with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and we'll sort it out.

Ads: what will I see, and how do I remove them?

On Android there's a small banner at the bottom, plus reward videos that only play when you choose to watch one (for coins, a hint, a key, or a one-hour try of a pack). After every 5 levels you finish, one full-screen video may show when you tap Next, never in the middle of a level. The Supporter pack or Complete pack removes all ads. Where consent applies, you can change it any time with Settings → Ad privacy choices. See the Privacy policy for details.

Can I play in dark mode?

Yes: Settings → Appearance. Auto follows your phone's dark mode, Dark and Light set it for every chapter. Themes from the Theme pack keep their own colours.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not at the moment. There's no account or cloud save, so progress, stars and coins are stored only on the device you play on. Purchased packs do carry over: use Restore purchases on the new phone.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings → Reset progress (tap it twice). This clears your levels, stars and earned coins on this device and can't be undone. Packs, keys and bought coins stay.

Any tips for the hard ones?
  • Start with the awkward corners and narrow bits: few pieces can fit there.
  • Count cells. The pieces cover the shape exactly (except the spare in Spare levels).
  • In Paint and Pegs, the coloured cells and pegs tell you a lot: place those pieces first.
  • Turn pieces in the tray (free) before dropping them, so you don't spend moves.
  • 12 clears open the next chapter, so a tough level never blocks you.
