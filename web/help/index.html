Playing Corral

The basics

Under the 9 × 9 board sit three wall pieces. Drag one onto the board and let go on empty cells; while you drag, a ghost shows where it will land (red where it can't). Pieces never rotate. When all three are used, three new ones arrive. If none of your pieces fits anywhere, the game is over.

Capturing a pen

When walls close off a patch of empty cells so that none of them can reach the edge of the board by stepping up, down, left or right through empty cells, that patch is captured: it flashes, fills with colour from the piece that closed it, and scores. Then every wall touching it (diagonally too) vanishes, freeing space. The board's edge is open, so a pen can never lean on it. Diagonal gaps don't leak: corners aren't needed.

Scoring

WhatPoints
Each wall cell placed1
A pen of area AA × A × 10 (1 cell 10, 4 cells 160, 9 cells 810, 16 cells 2,560)
Each gem inside the pen× 2 (two gems × 4, three × 8)
Two or more pens closed by one piece× 1.5 for two, × 2 for three

Because pens score their area squared, one big pen is worth far more than several small ones. Waiting for the right piece is a gamble: the board might fill up first. While you drag, the board previews any pen your piece would close and what it would score.

Rocks and gems

Rocks count as walls but never vanish. Gems sit in empty cells; pieces can't cover them, and a pen with a gem inside scores double. In Classic, gems appear now and then, rocks start to drop in as your score climbs, and later the gems hop to a neighbouring cell between trays.

Modes

  • Classic is endless. Pieces get more awkward as your score rises. The game always deals at least one piece that fits when any piece could, so you're never stuck by bad luck alone. Your best score is kept.
  • Daily Board is built from the date: everyone gets the same rocks, gems, starting walls and the same 30 pieces in the same order. Score for one to three stars; finishing it on consecutive days builds a streak (with bonus coins). Replays can raise your score for the day.
  • Challenges are 120 goals in 8 chapters, each with a fixed set of pieces: capture a pen of a given size, capture gems, close two pens at once, or reach a score. Meet the goal in fewer pieces for more stars. Clearing 10 challenges in a chapter opens the next one. Every challenge has been played through by a bot, so it can be done.

Hints, shuffles and the continue

  • Hint (30 coins; your first each day is free, three for Supporters) outlines a good spot for one of your pieces.
  • Shuffle (Classic, 50 coins) swaps your unused pieces for new ones.
  • Continue (Classic, once per run): when nothing fits, you can watch a short video or pay 150 coins to clear 14 walls near the middle and get three fresh pieces. It's only offered, never forced.
  • Out of coins? On Android you can watch a short video instead. The web version has no videos, so there it's free.

Coins, missions and awards

You earn coins from Classic scores (and a bonus for a new best), from clearing challenges and each new star, from finishing a chapter, from the Daily Board, from three daily missions, and from 39 awards. Coins pay for hints, shuffles and continues.

Shop

Everything in Corral opens by playing. The shop sells extras through Google Play: the Supporter pack (no ads, three free hints a day), the Theme pack (10 colour themes), Pen styles (6 looks for walls and pen colours), the Complete pack (all of it, plus 1,000 coins and 5 level keys), coin bags, level keys (open a locked challenge) and a tip jar. Themes and pen styles can be tried free for an hour with a video. Use Restore purchases in the shop after reinstalling.

Ads

On Android a small banner sits at the bottom of the screen, with its space kept free so it never covers the board. Rewarded videos play only when you ask for one. A short video plays after every fifth finished game, never in the middle of one. Supporters see no ads. The web version has no ads.

Saving

Your progress saves on this device after every move, so a Classic run picks up where you left it. Settings › Reset progress clears scores, stars, streaks, awards and earned coins, but keeps your settings, packs, keys and bought coins.
