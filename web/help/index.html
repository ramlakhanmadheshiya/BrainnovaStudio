Playing Mirrorlock

The basics

The board is split by a glowing mirror. Every piece is an arrow-shaped capsule, one to three cells long, and almost every piece has a twin: the piece in mirror image on the other side, in the same colour, pointing the mirrored way. Tap either twin and both slide straight ahead, in the direction they point, until they leave the board. Clear every piece to win.

Pressing a piece lights up its twin and draws a dotted line between them, so you can always check who moves with whom before you let go.

Bumps and hearts

A move is blocked if anything is in either twin's way: another piece, a stone, or a lock. Twins that slide toward each other across the mirror always crash in the middle. A blocked pair bumps forward, springs back, and you lose a heart. You have three hearts per level; with none left, try again, or spend 40 coins (or watch a video, on Android) for one more heart.

Stars

Clear a level with no bumps for three stars, with one bump for two, and with more for one. Using a hint makes two stars the most. The HEARTS / STARS strip above the board shows what is still on offer. Restart brings back every piece, heart and star; replay any level to improve.

The nine chapters

  • Reflect: a standing mirror down the middle: left and right swap.
  • Horizon: the mirror lies across the board: up and down swap.
  • Stones: a stone sits on one side only. Its number counts down with every move you make, and at zero it crumbles.
  • Solo: white pieces have no twin. They move alone and block only one side.
  • Slant: the mirror runs corner to corner: right becomes down and left becomes up.
  • Vault: a locked pair can't move until the pair carrying its key has escaped.
  • Kaleido: two mirrors at once: every piece has three twins, and all four move together.
  • Turn: the mirror turns a quarter after every move, so twins change as you play. A piece with no twin in the current mirror moves alone. Here the order matters: use Undo if you get stuck.
  • Mirrorlock: every mirror and every idea, mixed.

Hints, Undo and coins

  • Hint lights up a pair that can leave safely. You get 3 free hints a day (6 for Supporters), refilled at midnight; after that a hint costs 30 coins. Out of coins? On Android you can watch a short video for it instead.
  • Undo takes back your last move. It doesn't give back hearts.
  • Earn coins by clearing a level for the first time (+5), for each new star (+5), for finishing a chapter (+100), from the Daily Mirror (+30, more with a streak) and from awards. Rewards are paid once: replays only pay for new stars.

Chapters and levels

There are 9 chapters of 40 levels. In every chapter level 1 teaches the new idea, levels 1–5 are easy, 6–10 easy to medium, 11–20 medium, 21–30 medium to hard, 31–39 hard and level 40 extreme. Levels open one at a time, and clearing 15 levels in a chapter opens the next one. A level key opens any locked level early. A chapter key opens a whole locked chapter at once (starting at its level 1); you earn one by clearing level 40 of any chapter. Every level is built backwards from an empty board, so it can always be cleared, and checked by a solver.

Daily Mirror

A new puzzle every day, the same for every player. It is made on your device from the date, so it works offline. The mirror kind changes every day, Monday is the gentlest and Sunday the hardest.

Shop (Android)

Packs can be tried free for an hour with a video. Purchases go through Google Play; use Restore purchases after reinstalling.

Sharing

After you clear a level, tap Share on the result card to send a picture of the level with your stars and a link to the game, or Save it to your gallery.

Saving

Progress saves automatically on your device after every move, including a level you leave half way. In the browser, progress is stored by your browser for this site; clearing site data erases it. Nothing is uploaded.

Controls

Touch or mouse: tap a piece. Keyboard: H for a hint, Z to undo, R to restart, Esc to go back. On Android, the Back button works everywhere.
