Playing Word Lantern

The basics

Each level is a grid of letters on a theme, shown at the top (for example Fruit or Weather). Every tile belongs to exactly one word. Drag through neighbouring tiles (up, down, left or right, never diagonally) to spell a word, then let go. Correct words light up. Light every tile to clear the level.

The boxes above the grid show how many words there are and how long each one is (in the first two chapters, the words themselves are written in them). Words read in the direction you trace them. If you slide back onto the previous tile, the last letter is removed.

Stars

A perfect solve (no wrong words and no hints) earns three stars. Up to three extra moves (each wrong word or hint is one) earns two, and more earns one. The MOVES and STARS strip above the board shows what is still on offer. Restart puts all three stars back on offer; you can replay any level to improve.

Eight chapters, eight ideas

Each chapter adds one new idea, explained by a short card the first time you play it (tap How it works on the chapter's level screen to see it again).

  1. First Light: word list. The words are written out. Find each one in a straight line, across or down.
  2. Meadow: bends. The words are still listed, but now they turn corners and may read backwards.
  3. Harbor: hidden words. Only each word's length is shown. The theme is your clue.
  4. Orchard: shadows. Dark tiles hide their letters. Find any word touching a shadow and it lights up.
  5. Canyon: beacons. A tile with a small flame is a beacon; when its word is found, beams light every tile in its row and column.
  6. Bazaar: two themes. Two themes share every grid. Each theme's words sit on their own row above the grid.
  7. Moonwell: mystery theme. The theme is hidden until you find one of its words.
  8. Eclipse: everything. Hidden words, two mystery themes, shadows and beacons, all at once.

Hints and coins

  • Letter hint (25 coins) shows the next letter of a word. Your first letter hint each day is free (three for Supporters).
  • Word hint (60 coins) finds a whole word for you.
  • Out of coins? On Android you can watch a short video for that hint instead.
  • Earn coins by clearing a level for the first time (+5), for each new star (+5), for finishing a chapter (+100), from the daily lantern (+30, more with a streak) and from achievements. Rewards are paid once: replays only pay for new stars.

Chapters and levels

There are 8 chapters of 40 levels (320 in all). In every chapter levels 1–5 are easy, 6–10 easy to medium, 11–20 medium, 21–30 medium to hard, 31–39 hard and level 40 extreme. Levels open one at a time, and clearing 15 levels in a chapter opens the next one. A level key opens any locked level early. A chapter key opens a whole locked chapter at once (starting at its level 1); you earn one by clearing level 40 of any chapter. Every level is a little harder than the one before.

Shop (Android)

Everything opens by playing. The shop sells optional extras: the Supporter pack (no banner ads and no videos between levels, more free letter hints), colour themes, lantern styles, coins, level keys, and a tip jar. Packs can be tried free for an hour with a video. Purchases go through Google Play; use Restore purchases after reinstalling.

Daily lantern

A new puzzle every day, the same for every player. It is made on your device from the date, so it works offline. Monday is the gentlest and Sunday the hardest.

Sharing

After you clear a level, tap Share on the result card to send a picture of your solved puzzle with a link to the game, or Save it to your gallery.

Saving

Progress saves automatically on your device, including words found in a level you leave half way. In the browser, progress is stored by your browser for this site; clearing site data erases it. Nothing is uploaded.

Controls

Touch or mouse: drag to trace. Keyboard: H for a letter hint, R to restart, Esc to go back. On Android, the Back button works everywhere.
