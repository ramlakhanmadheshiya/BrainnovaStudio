Mirrorlock Help

Privacy policy

Your data stays on your device

Effective: 19 October 2026

Mirrorlock is a puzzle game for Android and the web, made by Ramlakhan Madheshiya (theramlakhan.com). In this policy, "we" means the developer. Questions? Email support@theramlakhan.com.

What's stored on your device

The game saves a small file with:

  • which levels you've cleared and your stars, and where you were in a level you left half way;
  • your coins, level keys and the levels they opened, chapter keys, daily streak and achievements;
  • your settings (music and sound volume, vibration, screen shake, appearance, colour theme and mirror style);
  • which purchases are unlocked, any video trials, and any tips you've given.

On Android this lives in the app's private storage; on the web it lives in your browser's storage for this site. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. We have no server that stores player data, no account system and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Ads (Android only)

The Android app uses Google AdMob to show a small banner at the bottom of the screen and, only if you choose to watch one, a rewarded video that gives you a hint or an extra heart when you are out of coins, free coins, a level key, a chapter key or a one-hour try of a cosmetic pack. There are no full-screen ads between levels. Supporters see no banner.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent. Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with the Ad privacy button in Settings.

Your advertising ID. On most Android phones open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.

Removing ads. The Supporter pack or Complete pack removes the banner. The web version never shows ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (no subscriptions) are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; we never see your card number or billing address. The app only receives confirmation of which item you bought and stores that unlock on your device. One-time packs are linked to your Google account, so you can get them back with Restore purchases in the shop.

Sharing

When you tap Share after clearing a level, the game makes a picture of the level on your device and opens your phone's share sheet with it and a link to the game. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself. Save puts the picture in your gallery (Pictures › Mirrorlock).

Third-party services in the app

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform: ads and ad consent, Android only.
  • Google Play Billing: purchases, Android only.
  • Godot Engine: the game engine. It collects nothing.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

Mirrorlock is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we'll help.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Reset progress clears your levels, stars, streaks, achievements and earned coins (packs, keys and bought coins are kept).
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes everything it saved.
  • On the web, clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your progress.

For data held by Google (ads or payments), use your Google Account settings.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top.
